ASV sequences constructed and selected by DADA2, with their taxonomy.
Only the merged sequences with a length higher than the threshold chosen to filter the non ribosomal sequences (config.yaml, dada2: seqlenth) and not assigned to the excluded taxa (taxonomy: exclude) are included in the file.
Download, drag and drop the file on `QIIME 2 View <https://view.qiime2.org/>`_
