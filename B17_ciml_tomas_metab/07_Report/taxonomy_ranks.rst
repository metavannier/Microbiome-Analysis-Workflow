Taxonomic levels of the classifier: for each level of the taxa bar plots and of the collapsed tables, the rank it holds, the number of ASVs assigned at that level and, among them, the ASVs with an informative name (named_ASVs: placeholders such as uncultured, metagenome, unidentified, Incertae_Sedis, "... bacterium", "... sp." and names copied from the upper rank are not counted). With the SILVA 144 classifiers the ranks are domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family and genus (level 7 = genus), plus the species (level 8) with the weighted classifiers; with SILVA 138, level 7 = species.
