Taxon abundance table at the species level (taxonomic level 7) formatted for `MetaboAnalyst <https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/>`_ ("Statistical Analysis [one factor]", samples in rows, unpaired): one row per sample, column Group = {{ snakemake.wildcards.column }}, then one column per taxon with its relative abundance in the sample (proportion of the reads of the sample over all taxa). Taxa absent from all the samples of the comparison are removed.
